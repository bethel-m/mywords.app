Community Guidelines

Last updated: 29th January 2025

Welcome to My words! To ensure a safe, respectful, and enjoyable experience for everyone, please adhere to these community guidelines when creating and sharing quotes and stories.

Failure to follow these guidelines may result in content removal, suspension, or account termination.

1. Respectful & Inclusive Content

  • Do not post content that promotes hate speech, discrimination, or violence against any individual or group based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, disability, or sexual orientation.
  • Avoid offensive language, slurs, or personal attacks.
  • Be kind and considerate when engaging with other users.

2. No Harmful, Misleading, or Illegal Content

  • Do not share false or misleading information that could harm individuals or the public.
  • Do not promote self-harm, suicide, eating disorders, or dangerous activities.
  • Do not encourage violence, criminal activities, or illegal drug use.
  • Any content related to terrorism, weapons, or extremist ideologies is strictly prohibited.

3. No Explicit or Adult Content

  • Nudity, pornography, or sexually explicit material is strictly prohibited.
  • Do not share sexually suggestive, violent, or graphic depictions in your quotes or stories.
  • Avoid romantic or suggestive content involving minors.

4. No Bullying, Harassment, or Threats

  • Do not post content that harasses, threatens, or shames individuals.
  • Avoid targeted cyberbullying or personal attacks.
  • Refrain from sharing private information (addresses, phone numbers, or personal details) of others.

5. No Copyrighted or Plagiarized Content

  • Only post original quotes and stories.
  • If using someone else’s work, ensure you have explicit permission or properly attribute it.
  • Do not post content that violates copyrights, trademarks, or intellectual property rights.

6. No Spam, Fake Engagement, or Impersonation

  • Do not post repetitive, irrelevant, or excessively promotional content.
  • Avoid fake accounts, impersonating others, or using misleading identities.
  • Do not engage in artificial engagement tactics (e.g., buying likes or followers).

7. Adherence to Local Laws & Google Play Policies

  • Content must comply with Google Play Store policies and local laws of the countries where our app is available.
  • Some topics may be restricted in certain regions due to legal or cultural differences.

8. Reporting Violations

  • If you encounter content that violates these guidelines, please report it through our in-app reporting tool or contact email.

9. Consequences of Violations

If a user violates these guidelines:

  1. First violation – A warning may be issued.
  2. Repeated violations – Content removal, temporary suspension, or account restrictions.
  3. Severe violations – Permanent account termination and possible legal action.

We reserve the right to update these guidelines as necessary.

By using My Words, you agree to follow these community standards and help us build a positive and respectful space.

🚀 Thank you for being part of our community!
